## Project intent
Create English-language, cinematic and clean documentaries about extreme technology, engineering, and science. Make complex systems feel tangible, legible, and consequential.

## Reference discipline
Supplied stills establish composition, lighting, palette, scale, and thumbnail typography only. They do not establish cut frequency, shot duration, narration, music, or transitions. Measure those from 3-5 complete videos and distinguish observation from interpretation and creative choice.

## Production rules
- Inspect real media and metadata before planning.
- Never invent source details, licenses, facts, quotes, or timing measurements.
- Log the source and rights status of each external asset.
- Final timeline timing follows measured approved narration audio; early scene timing is only provisional.
- Use integer timeline frames, explicit source in/out points, track assignments, and ordered clips.
- Preserve original media and work in an episode folder.
- Use only tools and integrations that are actually present. Probe CapCut, Computer Use, MCP, FFmpeg, and FFprobe before choosing an adapter.
- Do not silently substitute a render-only FFmpeg output for a native CapCut project.
- Keep unknown facts, assets, and rights visible as unresolved.
